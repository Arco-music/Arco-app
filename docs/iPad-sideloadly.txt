ARCO ON THE iPAD WITH SIDELOADLY (free, no Xcode)
1. On the Mac: download Sideloadly from sideloadly.io and install it.
2. Plug the iPad in with a cable, unlock it, tap Trust.
3. Open Sideloadly. Drag ARCO.ipa into it. Choose your iPad, type your Apple ID, press Start.
4. iPad: Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > your Apple ID > Trust.
   iPad (iOS 16+): Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > On (the iPad restarts).
5. Open ARCO. Works 7 days; Sideloadly can renew it (or do step 3 again).

THE SAME ARCO.ipa ON YOUR iPHONES (microphones + remote control)
1. Do the same steps with each iPhone plugged in (Sideloadly: choose the iPhone).
2. Open ARCO on the iPhone: it opens as a phone (no station window).
3. Open ARCO on the Mac (or iPad), same Wi-Fi (or the iPhone on the cable): SETUP > iPHONES shows it.
   Choose L1-L4: that phone's microphone plays as LIVE 1-4. Every phone is also a remote for the whole station.
4. Allow "Local Network" when the iPhone asks (it is how the station finds it).

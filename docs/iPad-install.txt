ARCO ON YOUR iPAD (or iPhone) - free, with your Apple ID
=======================================================

Once (about 20 minutes, mostly waiting for Xcode to download):

1. On the Mac, install Xcode from the Mac App Store (free). Open it once; when it asks which
   platforms to add, choose iOS.
2. Xcode menu > Settings > Accounts > "+" > Apple ID > sign in with your Apple ID.

Install ARCO:

3. Unzip ARCO-iPad-Xcode.zip. Open the folder ARCO-iPad > Builds > iOS and double-click ARCO.xcodeproj.
4. In Xcode, click the blue "ARCO" at the top of the left column, then "ARCO - App" under TARGETS,
   then the tab "Signing & Capabilities":
      - tick "Automatically manage signing"
      - Team: choose your name (Personal Team)
      - if Xcode complains about the Bundle Identifier, change it to com.yourname.arco
5. Plug the iPad into the Mac with a cable. Unlock it and tap "Trust".
   On the iPad: Settings > Privacy & Security > Developer Mode > On (the iPad restarts).
6. At the top of the Xcode window, choose your iPad as the device, then press the Play button.
   The first time, the iPad says the developer is not trusted:
   iPad Settings > General > VPN & Device Management > your Apple ID > Trust. Press Play again.
7. ARCO opens on the iPad. You can unplug the cable - ARCO works on its own.

Good to know:

- With a free Apple ID, ARCO keeps opening for 7 days. After that, plug the iPad in and press Play
  in Xcode again (1 minute). Your songs and settings stay.
- Songs and show recordings are in the Files app: On My iPad > ARCO.
- A microphone or audio interface: plug it into the iPad (USB-C, or Apple's Lightning to USB 3
  Camera Adapter). ARCO uses it; choose it in SETUP > AUDIO. Headphones go into the interface.
- iPhone: the same steps. ARCO opens on MIC FOR MAC (the iPhone as a microphone for ARCO on the Mac);
  "OPEN THE STATION" shows the full station.
- A new version: download the new ARCO-iPad-Xcode.zip and do steps 3, 4 and 6 again.
